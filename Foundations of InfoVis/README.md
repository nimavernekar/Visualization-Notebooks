# 🌍🏎️ Air Pollution & F1 Racing: A Dual Data Story

This notebook presents two compelling data narratives:

## 1️⃣ WHO Global Air Pollution Analysis

Using WHO's air quality dataset, we explore:
- NO₂ levels by WHO region and income group (violin plots)
- Top 10 countries with highest and lowest NO₂, PM2.5, and PM10 levels
- Visualizing pollution data on world maps using GeoPandas
- Pollution trends in specific cities (e.g., Delhi over time)

📁 **Dataset:**  
- WHO Air Quality Data (CSV)  
- WHO Country Income Groups (CSV)  
📌 Source: [WHO Air Database](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/themes/air-pollution/who-air-quality-database)

## 2️⃣ Formula 1 Racing Analytics (1950–2023)

Using the F1 dataset, this section reveals:
- Top 10 nationalities of F1 drivers
- Most-used circuits and races per season
- Most wins by driver (2022)
- Correlation between driver age and points earned
- Constructor team performance (2022)

📁 **Dataset:**  
- `drivers.csv`, `races.csv`, `results.csv`, `constructors.csv`, `lap_times.csv`, `standings.csv`

🎯 Key Techniques:
- Data merging & cleaning (Pandas)
- Exploratory visualizations with Seaborn and Matplotlib
- Geo-mapping pollution data with GeoPandas
- GroupBy, Aggregation, Violin/Line/Bar plots

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries
- Python: Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, GeoPandas
- Jupyter Notebook
- WHO Air Data + F1 Kaggle dataset
